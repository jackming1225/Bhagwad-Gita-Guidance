# Privacy Policy for Gita Wisdom

**Last Updated:** September 16, 2026

Welcome to **Gita Wisdom** ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, storage, and user privacy when you use our Android mobile application.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Selling or Commercial Exploitation
We believe spiritual reflection and personal introspection require absolute trust and sanctity.
- We **do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade** your personal information, journals, or search queries to any third parties or advertisers.
- We **do not** include any third-party advertising SDKs or tracking beacons.

---

### 2. Information We Store Locally on Your Device
Gita Wisdom is built as an **offline-first** application. The following data is stored purely on your local device via an encrypted local SQLite/Room database:
- **Daily Reflection Notes & Intentions**: Your personal contemplation notes and journal entries.
- **Reflection Streaks & History**: Timestamps marking completed days of reflection.
- **Favorite Verses & Bookmarks**: Verses you have starred or saved to personal collections.
- **Seeker Profile Preferences**: Your chosen display name, spiritual focus topic, and preferred guidance language.
- **Chat Conversation History**: Dialogue between you and the wisdom guide.

You can completely delete or reset this data at any time directly through the app settings or by clearing the app data in Android system settings.

---

### 3. Network Communications & AI Features
- **Internet Permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**:
  Used solely to connect to Google's Gemini Generative Language API for dynamic spiritual reflections when an active internet connection is present.
- **Transmission to Gemini API**:
  When you submit a spiritual dilemma or question, your query text and selected language are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS (TLS) to the Google Gemini API endpoint. This transmission is processed solely to generate philosophical reflections based on the Bhagavad Gita and is not retained by us.
- **Offline Fallback**:
  If network access is unavailable, the application processes all wisdom queries entirely offline on your device using its built-in repository of Bhagavad Gita verses.

---

### 4. Voluntary Dakshina / Contributions
The application includes a voluntary *Dakshina* offering feature honoring traditional Indian spiritual pedagogy.
- All contributions are 100% voluntary and do not unlock, restrict, or gate any spiritual content or application features.
- Transactions initiated via Unified Payments Interface (UPI) are handled directly by your installed UPI banking applications (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM). Gita Wisdom never collects, transmits, or stores your bank account details, UPI PIN, credit/debit card numbers, or payment credentials.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services
- **Google Generative AI (Gemini)**: For generating contextual spiritual counsel. Usage is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Android Text-To-Speech (TTS)**: For audio recitation of Sanskrit verses, utilizing your device's built-in system TTS engine.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
Gita Wisdom does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The content is suitable for general audiences seeking philosophical and ethical contemplation.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this policy. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
